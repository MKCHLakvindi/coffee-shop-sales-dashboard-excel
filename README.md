# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard in Excel

An interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze coffee shop sales performance, revenue trends, and profitability insights.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

This dashboard helps businesses:

- Track key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Analyze monthly and yearly revenue trends
- Compare revenue and profit performance
- Identify top-performing product categories
- Monitor city-wise sales performance
- Understand customer transaction behavior

---

## 🚀 Features

### ✅ Key Performance Metrics
- Total Transactions
- Total Quantity Sold
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit

### ✅ Category Transactions
Analyze sales distribution across:
- Tea Beverages
- Specialty Drinks
- Snacks
- Sandwiches
- Desserts
- Cold Drinks
- Coffee Beverages

### ✅ Weekly Transaction Trends
Identify busiest weekdays and customer purchasing behavior.

### ✅ Year-over-Year Revenue Comparison
Visual comparison of yearly revenue growth.

### ✅ Revenue by Category
Understand which product categories generate the highest revenue.

### ✅ Monthly Sales Overview
Track monthly transactions and performance trends.

### ✅ City-Wise Revenue Breakdown
Analyze sales contribution from:
- Austin
- Chicago
- Los Angeles
- New York
- Seattle

### ✅ Revenue vs Profit Analysis
Compare monthly revenue and profit for better business decision-making.
